# Personal Edit Mode

This mode is intended for players who just want to dabble with and edit the in-game art for their own personal reasons. 

Turn on **Personal Edit Mode**, open the Compendium, and click any card. The editor panel appears on the left of the enlarged card.

## Standard tab

- **Tint:** click the swatch to pick a color the whole image is multiplied by. White = unchanged.
- **Hue:** rotates every color around the color wheel.
- **Sat:** color intensity. 0 is grayscale.
- **Lum:** brightness.
- **Gamma:** darkens or lifts the midtones without touching pure black and white.
- **Red, Green, Blue:** multiply that color channel.
- **Red, Green, Blue Offset:** add or remove that color evenly, shadows included, which the multiply sliders cannot reach.
- **Contrast:** pushes darks and lights apart, or flattens them together.
- **Flip X / Flip Y:** mirror the art left to right, or top to bottom.

Order of operations: the color channels and offsets, then Hue, Sat and Lum, then Contrast, Gamma and Tint.

## Selective Hue tab

Changes one color in the art and leaves everything else alone, such as turning a blue flame purple. There are three independent sets.

1. Click a set's **Target** swatch, then press the **eyedropper** button in the color picker and click the color you want to change on the card's own art.
2. Raise or lower **Shift** to rotate that color to a new one, and use **Saturation** and **Brightness** to mute, intensify, darken or lighten it.
3. Adjust **Width** until the change catches all of that color and nothing else. Only the hue is compared, so darker and paler versions of the color are caught too.
4. Raise **Softness** if the change leaves a hard edge or a visible fringe.

**Reset** beside a set's swatch picker turns just that set off.

The sets apply after the whole Standard tab, in order: Set 1, then Set 2, then Set 3.

## Bottom panel

- **Search portrait paths:** replace the card's art with any other card's. Type part of a name and pick a result.
- **Save:** keeps your changes for this card. Until you save, everything is only a preview.
- **Clear:** deletes your saved changes, so the card goes back to its normal art.
- **Copy / Paste:** copy one card's colors and flips onto another. The art itself is not copied.
- **Reset:** puts everything back to neutral and shows the card's original art.

Saved changes apply everywhere the card appears, on your computer only, and survive restarts and mod updates.
